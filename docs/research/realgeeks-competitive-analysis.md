# Real Geeks Competitive Analysis

_Last updated: 2026-06-05._

## Why this matters

John Ilao reported that the majority of Guam brokers may use or know Real Geeks, and Carl from the MLS committee specifically said Real Geeks is who Hafa Homes would be up against when pitching brokers.

That means the competitive set is not simply Zillow, Locations LLC, or generic brokerage websites. For broker sales, the real competitive question is:

> Can Hafa Homes provide a better Guam-specific version of the brokerage website + IDX + app + lead CRM stack that Real Geeks sells nationally?

## Real Geeks capability map

### 1. IDX websites

Real Geeks promotes custom-branded IDX websites for agents/teams/brokers.

Observed claims/features:

- IDX real estate websites.
- Branded property search.
- Lead capture forms.
- Search behavior tracking.
- Customizable MLS feeds.
- Multiple MLS feeds as optional/add-on.
- SEO-oriented pages.
- Website launch quickly, e.g. “14 days or less” / “live in 2 weeks or less” messaging.
- Custom capture forms.
- Neighborhood/market pages.

### 2. CRM / lead manager

Real Geeks CRM is framed as the relationship hub.

Observed claims/features:

- Manage active leads.
- Track lead searches and activity.
- Lead feed / hot lead surfacing.
- Notes and follow-up workflows.
- Automated email/SMS drip workflows.
- Sign-up autoresponder texts.
- Property and market alerts.
- CRM mobile app.
- Agent, lender, and ISA users.
- Integrations.

### 3. Mobile app / MoveTo

Real Geeks offers a branded consumer search app called MoveTo.

Observed claims/features:

- Branded app experience.
- Consumers can search, save, share, and take action.
- Real-time alerts for saved searches.
- Smart location search.
- Favorites/searches sync across website and app.
- App activity is tracked in the CRM.

### 4. Lead quality / verified leads

Real Geeks has “Geek Verified Leads.”

Observed claims/features:

- Leads can confirm their phone number during registration.
- Verified badge/status appears in CRM.
- Unverified leads are still captured.
- Unverified users can continue browsing, but access to additional photos may be limited until phone confirmation.
- The purpose is to prioritize reachable leads and reduce fake/unreachable contacts.

### 5. Seller leads / valuation

Real Geeks offers a property valuation tool / EstateIQ.

Observed claims/features:

- Seller lead capture.
- Mobile-friendly valuation UX.
- Captures property info, condition, and listing intent.
- Sends ready-to-contact seller leads into the pipeline.
- Encourages seller interactions with CTA prompts.

### 6. Marketing / lead generation

Real Geeks offers marketing products and lead-generation packages.

Observed claims/features:

- Facebook Ad Tool.
- Google/Facebook PPC management.
- Marketing Studio.
- Property search advertising.
- Property social advertising.
- Lead retargeting add-ons.
- Listing boost ads.
- Market reports.
- Lead guarantee on some packages.

### 7. Dialer / communications

Real Geeks offers a built-in dialer and SMS autoresponders.

Observed claims/features:

- Call from lead record.
- Log call outcomes.
- Keep notes and tracking inside CRM.
- Automated speed-to-lead SMS.
- Respond to lead registration and website activity automatically.

### 8. Pricing / packaging

Real Geeks public pricing page emphasizes tiers rather than fixed prices.

Observed package names:

- Establish
- Grow
- Expand
- Conquer

Observed included/varied elements:

- CRM users.
- IDX website.
- Custom-branded property search app.
- Property alerts and market reports.
- Property valuation experience.
- Lead conversion optimization.
- Marketing tools/leads.
- Dedicated success manager on higher tiers.
- Additional users have monthly per-user pricing.

## What Real Geeks teaches us

### Brokers buy outcomes, not just UI

Real Geeks sells:

- better lead capture
- better follow-up
- more reachable leads
- agent/team accountability
- marketing support
- search/app/website as one funnel

Therefore Hafa Homes cannot pitch only “better map search.” The broker pitch needs to include lead conversion and CRM workflow.

### Broker website takeover is part of the product

If a broker uses Hafa Homes but keeps an unrelated Real Geeks or legacy website, the consumer funnel is split. Brokers may reasonably ask Hafa Homes to power the website too.

### Quality leads are a must-have conversation

Real Geeks explicitly invests in lead verification. Guam brokers will likely care about:

- fake/incomplete contact info
- duplicate leads
- agent follow-up speed
- which leads are ready/reachable
- whether lead sources are worth paying for

### Hafa Homes should not try to clone everything first

Real Geeks is broad. Hafa Homes should start with the Guam-specific version of the highest-value pieces:

1. brokerage website/app branding
2. listing/search UX
3. lead inbox/CRM basics
4. lead routing and accountability
5. saved homes/searches and lead activity
6. local Guam intelligence
7. property-management/rental workflows later

## Hafa Homes competitive thesis

Real Geeks is broad and established. Hafa Homes can win locally by being:

- Guam-first, not generic mainland real estate software.
- Faster to customize for broker workflows.
- Better at local village, military, rental, and property-management context.
- Able to provide a polished mobile app experience and broker-specific app branding.
- A local partner with Mike/John/Leon relationships and support.
- Flexible enough to integrate with Real Geeks first if a broker is not ready to switch CRM.

## Positioning against Real Geeks

Avoid saying:

> “We are a cheaper Real Geeks clone.”

Better:

> “Real Geeks is a national IDX/CRM platform. Hafa Homes is a Guam-first brokerage website, app, and lead platform built around Guam search behavior, local broker workflows, and the rental/property-management realities of this market.”

## Feature comparison: Real Geeks vs Hafa Homes target

| Area | Real Geeks | Hafa Homes target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IDX/listing website | Strong incumbent | Broker-branded Guam IDX/listing website, subject to MLS approval |
| Consumer app | MoveTo app | Hafa Homes demo app + broker-branded app builds from shared Expo codebase |
| CRM | Mature CRM | Start focused: lead inbox, status, assignment, notes, timeline, tasks |
| Lead verification | Phone verification / badges | Add email/phone verification and lead quality badges later |
| Marketing | PPC/Facebook/retargeting packages | Start with source tracking and simple campaign links; add ads later or integrate partners |
| Market reports | Built-in | Guam village/market reports later if MLS data allows |
| Seller valuation | EstateIQ | Future seller valuation / home estimate lead form, compliance-aware |
| Local Guam context | Generic national platform | Major differentiator: village intel, military relocation, rentals, typhoon-ready features |
| Property management | Not core public emphasis | Potential premium differentiator for Guam broker/property managers |
| Local support | National vendor | Local Guam/Shimizu support and custom workflow adaptation |

## Discovery questions for brokers using Real Geeks

- What do you like most about Real Geeks?
- What is frustrating about it?
- Do agents actually use the CRM?
- How many leads are fake/unreachable?
- How quickly are leads contacted?
- How are leads assigned between agents?
- Does Real Geeks handle rentals/property management well enough for Guam?
- Would you replace your website if the Hafa Homes-powered site/app/CRM handled the same basics?
- Would you prefer integration with your current CRM before replacement?
- What would make you switch?
