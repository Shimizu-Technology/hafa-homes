# Guam Market Research

## Existing Guam real estate websites

Research found several Guam real estate websites already providing MLS/IDX-style search. Most appear to be brokerage/team lead-generation websites rather than polished consumer housing platforms.

## Notable examples

### GuamHome.com

Features observed:

- Search Guam real estate
- For sale and for rent
- City/village filters
- Beds, baths, price, property type
- Advanced search
- Address search
- Open houses
- Home evaluation
- Saved account/login flow
- Instant MLS access and listing alerts
- Popular searches:
  - Near Andersen AFB / Camp Blaz
  - Near Navy Base / Naval Hospital
  - Pools
  - Ocean view
  - Condo living
  - Downtown living
  - New construction
  - Luxury
  - Pet friendly
  - North/Central/South Guam

### GuamRealtyFinder.com

Features observed:

- Sale/rent search
- MLS number search
- Houses, condos/townhouses, land, multiplexes, commercial, businesses
- Location filters including:
  - Beachfront
  - Ocean view
  - Tumon Bay
  - Near Andersen
  - Near Navy Base
  - Near Navy Hospital
- Renting, military rentals, financing, and services content

### LivingOnGuam.com

Features observed:

- Guam MLS search
- Saved searches/favorite properties
- North/Central/South Guam entry points
- Military content
- Market report
- Home valuation CTA
- Strong relocation positioning

### Other relevant sites

- Platinum Guam
- Signature Realty Guam
- RE/MAX Guam sites
- Guam Is Hot

## Market gap

The gap is not lack of listings. The gap appears to be:

- Modern mobile-first UX
- Strong app-like experience
- Cohesive Guam housing brand
- Better saved search/alerts presentation
- Better map/list exploration
- Better local content integration
- Better rental and military workflows
- Better investor-ready platform story

## Guam-specific opportunities

### Military relocation

Guam has a major opportunity around PCS and military housing search.

Useful filters/content:

- Andersen AFB
- Naval Base Guam
- Camp Blaz
- Naval Hospital
- OHA-friendly rentals
- Commute estimates
- Furnished rentals
- Short-term arrival housing
- Pet-friendly rentals

### Local property features

Guam housing search should highlight features that matter locally:

- Typhoon shutters
- Generator
- Water tank
- Split AC
- Fenced yard
- Gated community
- Covered parking
- Pet policy
- Ocean view
- Pool
- Furnished/unfurnished
- Utilities included

### Village-based search

Village identity matters. The product should support village pages and region grouping:

- North Guam
- Central Guam
- South Guam
- Tamuning/Tumon
- Dededo/Yigo
- Mangilao/Barrigada

### Rentals

Rentals may be an especially strong wedge because renters, military families, and property managers need fast, current information.

Potential rental-specific features:

- Availability date
- Lease length
- Pets allowed
- Furnished
- Utilities included
- Yard/fence
- OHA-friendly
- Property manager contact
- Application/request viewing flow

## Competitive positioning

Hafa Homes should be positioned as a polished, local-first housing app, not just another IDX site.

Suggested message:

> Search Guam homes and rentals with local filters that actually matter — village, base commute, pets, furnished rentals, typhoon-ready features, and trusted local support.
