## High-level outcome

Leon, Mike, and John aligned around partnering on Hafa Homes as the first focused product under a broader **SSI Automation** / Shimizu Technology collaboration.

The meeting reframed Hafa Homes from only a Guam consumer real-estate search app into a **brokerage-focused software platform**:

> A polished mobile app and web/admin platform that Guam brokerages can subscribe to for listing search, lead capture, agent engagement, and eventually property management / tenant workflows.

The group agreed that real estate is the best immediate vertical to push to the finish line before expanding the same playbook into other Guam industries.

## Product direction after the meeting

### Core strategy

Hafa Homes should become a **broker-first platform**, not just an agent-by-agent tool.

Rationale:

- Brokers have larger budgets than individual agents.
- Brokers already subscribe/pay for MLS/Flexmls access.
- Brokers can authorize or provide listing data.
- Brokers can route leads to agents.
- Brokers can use the app/platform as a recruiting, retention, and conversion tool.

### MLS / data access understanding

The group aligned that Hafa Homes likely cannot directly access MLS by itself at first.

Working assumption:

- MLS/Flexmls access is broker-mediated.
- The participating broker authorizes Hafa Homes or provides the needed feed/access.
- For now, Leon can keep building with demo/seed/test listing data.
- Real MLS/feed integration becomes part of onboarding a broker customer.

Open questions:

- Exact Flexmls/MLS feed type available to Guam brokers.
- Whether a broker can authorize a third-party app/vendor.
- Whether Hafa Homes can display all IDX-approved listings or only broker/customer listings.
- Required attribution, disclaimers, photo rights, refresh rules, and lead-routing rules.

## Product packaging

The current packaging hypothesis is:

### Tier 1 — Brokerage App / Listing Search

Purpose: entry-level broker-branded or broker-supported app/search experience.

Potential features:

- Brokerage profile/branding.
- Listing search and filters.
- Map search.
- Listing detail pages.
- Agent/broker attribution.
- MLS/listing feed integration once available.
- Basic lead/contact capture.

### Tier 2 — Engagement / Lead Platform

Purpose: make the app useful for conversion, not just browsing.

Potential features:

- Direct showing requests.
- Messaging/contact workflows.
- Saved/favorited listings.
- Scheduling appointment/showing requests.
- Mortgage/finance calculator.
- Local Intel / neighborhood context.
- Lead inbox and lead status tracking.
- Agent/broker lead routing.

### Tier 3 — Property Management / Tenant Portal

Purpose: premium package for brokerages or property managers with rental portfolios.

Potential features:

- Managed properties.
- Tenant accounts.
- Lease/file uploads.
- Contract/lease date tracking.
- Rent-payment portal.
- Payment status/history.
- Messaging between property manager and tenant.
- Maintenance/request workflow later.

Important product decision:

- The broker-facing/admin/property-management workflows should probably live primarily on the web dashboard because admins will use desktop.
- The mobile app remains focused on consumer search, saved listings, showing/contact, and tenant-friendly lightweight tasks.

## Pricing discussion

No final pricing was decided.

Numbers discussed as rough research/hypothesis:

- Real estate agent software can range around **$30–$300+ per agent/month**.
- Broker setup fees could potentially range **$1,000–$5,000**, depending on package and onboarding complexity.
- Monthly broker subscription could be tiered. Rough possibilities discussed included:
  - lower recurring maintenance/support fees for basic packages
  - higher monthly fees for premium broker/property-management packages
  - pricing can be justified by number of agents, lead value, and operational savings

Strategic pricing notes:

- Avoid underpricing just because initial software overhead is low.
- Broker value is not only hosting; it is lead generation, automation, app presence, reduced friction, and ongoing support.
- Pricing and proposals are Mike/John homework items while Leon continues product buildout.
