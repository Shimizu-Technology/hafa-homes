# 2026-06-05 Broker Feedback: Real Geeks, Broker Websites, CRM

_Last updated: 2026-06-05._

## Source

WhatsApp update from John Ilao / Mike Sakazaki after conversations with Guam real estate contacts.

Key messages captured:

- John: “Need to research Real Geeks. Majority of the brokers use this.”
- John met with Carl, who sits on the MLS committee. Carl said Real Geeks is a major incumbent we would be up against when pitching brokers.
- Main broker priorities mentioned: **quality leads** and **CRM**.
- Carl recommended speaking with Clare Delgado from Home Ventures because she has long-time market insight.
- Carl also recommended reaching out to Bawar, current president of GAR.
- Mike added that brokers may want Hafa Homes/Shimizu to take over their actual brokerage website too, because a separate app plus a separate existing website may not make sense operationally or from a branding perspective.

## Interpretation

This is an important strategy update.

The broker opportunity is not just:

> “Can we make a better Guam listing app?”

It is closer to:

> “Can we provide a Guam-first brokerage website, app, lead platform, and lightweight CRM that replaces or augments what brokers currently get from tools like Real Geeks?”

Hafa Homes should remain the demo/reference product, but the sellable platform should support brokerage-specific websites, brokerage-specific app builds, and broker/agent CRM workflows.

## Immediate implications

1. **Hafa Homes becomes the demo platform.**
   - It proves the mobile search UX, Local Intel, map/search, account, saved listings, and showing request flows.
   - It can also remain a public Guam discovery layer.

2. **Brokerage customers may want their own branded site/app.**
   - We should expect requests like `brokerage.com`, `search.brokerage.com`, and “Brokerage Name” iOS/Android app.
   - They may not want a generic Hafa Homes consumer app as the only consumer surface.

3. **The CRM/lead system matters as much as the consumer UI.**
   - Brokers care about lead quality, routing, speed-to-lead, agent accountability, and whether leads turn into conversations.

4. **MLS/Flexmls/GAR relationship discovery is urgent.**
   - Carl, Clare Delgado, and Bawar are important discovery contacts for MLS committee rules, GAR context, broker pain points, and Real Geeks usage.

## Follow-up questions for Carl / Clare / Bawar

### MLS / data access

- What MLS/Flexmls products do Guam brokers currently use for IDX/search?
- Does Real Geeks integrate through Flexmls IDX, RESO Web API, RETS, iframe/embed, CSV, or another approved method?
- Can a local vendor like Hafa Homes be authorized by one brokerage? Multiple brokerages?
- What listing/photo attribution and disclaimer rules apply?
- Are there rules around registration walls, saved searches, sold data, refresh cadence, or photo caching?

### Broker website / CRM usage

- Which brokerages use Real Geeks today?
- Are they satisfied with Real Geeks? Where does it fall short?
- Do brokers feel they “own” their leads, website, and data?
- Are leads high quality/reachable?
- What happens after a lead comes in? Who calls? How quickly? How are statuses tracked?
- Do agents use the CRM consistently, or does it become shelfware?
- Do brokers want mobile admin/agent tools, or mostly desktop web?

### Website takeover

- Would brokers consider replacing their public website if the app/search/CRM is better?
- Do they need custom content pages: agents, rentals, property management, buyer/seller guides, neighborhoods, military relocation?
- How important are SEO and IDX neighborhood pages?
- Do brokers want their own app-store app, or is a Hafa Homes-powered app acceptable if branded?

## Strategic decision

Hafa Homes should evolve into a **white-label-capable brokerage platform** while preserving Hafa Homes as the reference/demo brand.

Recommended positioning:

> Hafa Homes is the Guam-first real estate platform that powers brokerage websites, brokerage apps, lead CRM, and local property search experiences.
